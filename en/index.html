What we automate

Most work that is done often, the same way, and by hand can be built away. We start from your actual workflows and the systems you already have.

Invoices & bookkeeping

Parsing, coding and export into your accounting system (Fortnox, Visma, Xero, QuickBooks). Reconciliations that take care of themselves.

Orders & quotes

Registration, calculations and confirmations, from email and web forms straight into your business system.

Email & cases

Incoming mail is sorted, summarised and given ready-made draft replies. A human approves before anything is sent.

Integrations

The systems you already run start talking to each other: business system, point of sale, web shop, bank.

Reporting

Daily figures compiled and sent where they belong, without anyone copying and pasting.

AI agents

For unstructured work (text, documents, research) we build agent systems with clear controls and traceability.

Deterministic where it can be, AI where it helps. Rules and integrations when the answer must come out exactly the same every time; language models when the work involves unstructured text, always with a human who approves.

How we work

Management & further development

The system grows with the business. You own the value and the everyday work; we handle the technology and build further where it helps most.

Start with a pilot

The first step should be small and concrete. We take one of your flows, put it into operation and show in plain numbers what it saves, before you commit to anything larger. We run a couple of pilots at a time.

One AI coworker, in operation

We pick one clear, recurring task (for example supplier invoices into your accounting system) and build a digital coworker for exactly that, with your systems and under your review.

Fixed pilot price

A defined price for the setup and the first weeks of management. No large investment before you have seen the value, no open-ended hours.

Weekly report with numbers

Every week you get a simple report: cases handled, hours saved, and what that is worth in money. The value is measurable from the first week.

If you see no clear value, we do not go further. The pilot is built to prove the value, not to lock you in. If it works, it becomes an ongoing AI coworker that we manage and build further on.
